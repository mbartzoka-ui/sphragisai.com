AI Security Assessment

A security assessment for chatbots, AI agents and LLM-based applications: what can go wrong, what can leak, and how to protect them.

2–4 weeks, depending on scopeOn request
Free 20′ session

Who it is for

  • Businesses with a chatbot on their website, e-shop or customer service
  • Teams building AI agents with access to systems and data
  • Anyone who wants security evidence before releasing an AI system

What it includes

Model manipulation

Prompt injection and jailbreak tests: can someone make the system ignore its rules?

Data leakage

Can the system reveal internal instructions, personal data or customer information?

Accuracy and behaviour

Wrong or harmful answers, promises that bind the business, off-topic conversations.

Protective measures

Recommendations for guardrails, configuration, monitoring and what to do when something goes wrong.

FAQ

Will the live system be affected?

Tests are designed not to affect customers and run only with your written approval.

Do you need access to the code?

Not necessarily. Testing can be done from the user side; with access to the configuration it is more complete.
