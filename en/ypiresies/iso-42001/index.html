AI Management System under ISO/IEC 42001

ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for responsible and secure use of AI. We build with you a system that works in practice and prepares you for the certification audit.

Usually 3–6 months to the certification auditOn request
Free 20′ session

Want to understand the standard first? The ISO/IEC 42001 guide →

Who it is for

  • Companies developing or embedding AI in products and services
  • Businesses that need to prove to clients and tenders that they manage AI responsibly
  • Organisations preparing for EU AI Act obligations

What it includes

Scope and AI policy

Which systems are covered, roles and responsibilities, the company's AI policy.

Risk and impact assessment

For each AI system: what can go wrong, who it affects (the organisation, people, society) and how we reduce it.

Controls and documentation

The standard's Annex A controls, the Statement of Applicability and procedures, sized to you.

Internal audit and management review

A full check before the certification body arrives, and the management meeting the standard requires.
