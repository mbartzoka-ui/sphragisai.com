Frequently asked questions

Does the test mean the assistant will never make a mistake?

No. It certifies what was tested, when, with what result and who approved it. No AI system is infallible. That is why there are filters on every conversation, re-testing and the option to correct answers. The test does not cover the security of the rest of your site.

What are credits?

Each answer uses credits depending on the model you choose: 1 credit for the standard model (Claude Haiku), 10 for the more accurate one (Claude Sonnet), 20 for the top one (Claude Opus). The standard model is enough for most questions. The more accurate ones give more careful answers on complex topics. You can switch models at any time and see usage in the admin panel.

Where are conversations stored?

In a database in the EU, without IP addresses, and deleted automatically after 90 days or whatever period you set. Form details are encrypted. Answers are written by Anthropic’s Claude models, which do not use the messages for training.

Do I need a developer?

No. Installation is one line of code or a plugin for WordPress and PrestaShop. We do the configuration, and afterwards you can change anything from the admin panel.
