Why now

When the EU AI Act binds you

The regulation applies in phases. Since 2 August 2026, the transparency obligations of Article 50 apply to chatbots and voice agents.

FAQ
I just use a chatbot from an off-the-shelf platform. Does the AI Act apply to me?

Most likely, yes. As a deployer you have your own obligations, such as disclosing that the AI is an AI and keeping human oversight. You remain responsible to your customers for what the bot says, whatever the platform contract states.

Do I need you or a lawyer?

Both, for different things. We map the system, its gaps and risks technically, so your lawyer can work in a targeted way. We do not give legal advice, and every contract finding goes through legal review.

Can you guarantee that I will be compliant?

No. AI systems carry inherent uncertainty and the regulation keeps evolving. We offer measurable risk reduction: what you have, where you are exposed, what to fix first and what documentation to keep.

How fast do we start?

The Shielding Check starts within the week and is delivered in 5–7 business days. It is preceded by a free 45-minute session where we define the exact scope.

Do you assess systems you built yourselves?

No. An assessment is only worth something if it is impartial, so we never review our own work.
