New 'Compaction Smuggling' technique bypassed OpenAI's defenses against stolen model reasoning

Security firm Xint said it found a flaw in OpenAI's compaction process, the step where a conversation's accumulated history is compressed into a summary before it overflows a model's context window, that allowed a model's hidden internal reasoning to be moved into another model, a technique that could significantly cut the cost of building a rival AI through distillation (training a smaller model to mimic a larger one's abilities).
